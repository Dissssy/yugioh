# Duel niceties

Status, 2026-11-24: Ethan asked for sound effects, desktop notifications and easier switching between duels, and
approved every extra idea below. Each step is its own PR and works on its own.

| Step | What | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Your duels, live.** One feed per player (`GET /api/me/duels/stream`) sends a duel's summary to both its players whenever it may have changed. `DuelSummary.yourMove` says what the duel waits on you for: `deck`, `turn` or `answer` (a response you can make, a trigger, a pick, a judge's question, a rollback offer). Windows the server passes by itself for you don't count. The top bar (and the duel screen's panel, which has no top bar) shows your other live duels as chips, lit while they wait on you. **G** opens the next one waiting on you. The tab title counts them: "(2) Yu-Gi-Oh Duels". | Built |
| 2 | **Sound effects**, generated in code with ZzFX (Ethan chose generated now, recorded CC0 samples maybe later; the sound table is one file). One sound per board event, a chime for your turn and a ping when you need to answer. Mute and volume per browser, next to animation speed; the speaker button or **M** mutes. Sound starts after the first click or key press, as browsers require. Code: `web/src/sound.ts` (the table), `soundOf`/`awaitsAnswer` in `web/src/board/cues.ts`. | Built |
| 3 | **Desktop notifications** from the same feed, from any page or a background tab: your turn, an answer needed, a challenge accepted, your opponent ready. Not for the duel you're looking at. Hidden in the Discord Activity, whose frame can't ask for permission. The switch is in Account and the duel settings (guests have no Account page), saved per browser. Any of these news also pings, unless you're watching that duel's board, which makes its own sounds. Code: `web/src/notify.tsx`. | Built |
| 4 | **Web Push**, so notifications arrive with the site closed: asynchronous dueling. Turning on desktop notifications also subscribes the browser (`web/public/sw.js`, `PUT /api/me/push`). The feed (`server/src/duelFeed.ts`) pushes a player's news only while no tab of theirs follows it, so an open tab and a push never both fire; the news text is shared (`shared/src/duelNews.ts`). Needs `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` and `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` ([deploy.md](deploy.md)); without them it stays off. Response windows you can't act in pass by themselves, so a push means you really have something to do. | Built |
| 5 | **Rematch** at the end of a duel with the same decks, shaped to grow into best of three. `POST /api/duels/:id/rematch` makes (or joins) one new duel, `matches.rematch_of` pointing back, with the same players, seats, banlist and decks, and readies the asker; accepting is the same call, and the duel starts once both are ready (an AI opponent is ready at once). Either player can change their deck on the rematch's pre-duel page first, which is where a best of three's side decking will happen; a series is the chain of `rematch_of` links. Both boards move to the rematch when it starts. Code: `web/src/board/Rematch.tsx`, `Duels.rematch`. | Built |
| 6 | **Opponent online dot** and an **unread chat badge** with a soft sound. A duel's stream sends `presence` (which seats have it open) after the sync and whenever it changes; the opponent's LP plate shows a green dot while they're there (both plates for a spectator, none in a replay). The opponent's chat plays a soft sound, and messages that arrived while the log's end was out of sight or the tab hidden show as "N new messages ↓", which scrolls to them. | Built |
| 7 | **Quick emotes** (Good game, Thinking…, Nice play) with their own sounds, and **phone vibration** on your turn. | Planned |

Deferred: a "voice" for text, like animal babble, maybe only for chat and short judge rulings.
